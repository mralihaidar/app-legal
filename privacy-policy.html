PRIVACY POLICY

Secure Charging Animations

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Secure Charging Animations ("the App", "we", "us") is built to work great without asking much of you. This page explains, in plain language, what data the App and the services it relies on can access, what we do with it, and what stays entirely on your device.

01 The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. You can use the App without ever giving us your name, email, or phone number.
  • Face ID / PIN data never leaves your device. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's on-device Face ID system — the App only ever receives a yes/no result, never your face data. Your PIN is stored locally in the device Keychain.
  • We don't run our own tracking servers. Any data collection described below comes from well-known third-party services (ads, subscriptions, crash/config tooling), not a backend we operate ourselves.
  • You control ad tracking. On first launch, the App asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and shows a consent form (required for users in the EEA/UK). You can decline both and continue using the App normally.

02 Information we collect

Information you provide

The App does not require registration. The only information you actively provide is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us:

  • Your chosen PIN (stored in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the OS)
  • Your app preferences — selected animation, theme, language, notification settings
  • Intruder detection logs (timestamps of failed unlock attempts), stored locally only

Information collected automatically

Some third-party services the App relies on may automatically collect standard technical data — device type, OS version, general (non-precise) region, advertising identifiers (IDFA, where permitted), and app usage/crash diagnostics — to serve ads, process purchases, and keep the App stable. See the table below for exactly which services and why.

04 How we use information

  • To show and personalize ads (only with your consent)
  • To process and validate Pro subscription purchases
  • To remotely adjust non-essential app behavior (e.g. ad frequency) without an app update
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability

We do not sell your personal information. We do not build cross-app profiles of you ourselves — any advertising profile is built and controlled by the ad networks listed above, per their own policies and your consent choices.

05 Your choices & controls

  • Ad tracking: Decline or reset anytime via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Settings → [App Name] → Tracking.
  • Ad personalization (EEA/UK): Change your consent anytime from the in-app Privacy Options entry.
  • Notifications: Enable or disable charger-event and daily reminders anytime in Settings, in-app or in iOS Settings.
  • PIN / Face ID: Enable, disable, or change your lock method anytime from the app's Security settings — even if you skipped setup at first launch.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (PIN, logs, preferences) from your device.

06 Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's ad configuration flags non-child-directed treatment to our ad partners.

07 Data retention

Locally stored data (PIN, logs, preferences) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data held by third-party services (ad networks, RevenueCat, Firebase) is retained per their own policies — see the links below.

08 International users

If you're in the EEA, UK, or another region with its own data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data held about you by the third-party processors above. Requests about App-level local data can be resolved simply by adjusting Settings or uninstalling the App; requests about third-party processor data should go directly to that provider, or to us and we'll help route it.

09 Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes, and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App's "What's New" release notes.
